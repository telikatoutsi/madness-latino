Si el latino es tu idioma, esta es tu casa.

Madness Latino is a reggaeton night that started in Trondheim in December 2023. One DJ, one room, and a crowd that came for perreo and stayed until the lights went on.

Since then it has moved into Hjorten Scene at the Radisson Blu Royal Garden and gone on the road: Oslo, Ålesund, Haugesund and Stavanger. Numbered volumes through the year, plus San Valentine, the Summer Party and Halloween Madness.

Behind the decks

DJ Tricky Ricky

Ricky is from Venice, and he did not stay put. He travelled the world: some years in Australia, time in Prague, time in Peru. Every stop added records to the bag and another way to read a room.

Now he is behind the decks at Madness Latino, playing reggaeton, dembow and Latin hits to the crowd in front of him.
